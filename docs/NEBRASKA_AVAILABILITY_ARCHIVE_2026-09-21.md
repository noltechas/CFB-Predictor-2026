# Nebraska availability archive (2026-09-21)

This pass uses the pinned schedule at `models/player_sequence_production/refreshes/20260920T122506160000Z/source/snapshot_schedule.parquet` (SHA-256 `43860af01716c167a410e95d1c0f1702d03a2863983c77c6b26a12077e8c6f71`). It enumerates every Nebraska fixture in that snapshot: 313 games from 2001 through 2026, including 304 completed games and nine future 2026 games. The complete fixture ledger is `data/raw/availability_backfill/nebraska_20260921/all_nebraska_games.json` and `.csv`.

The ledger is intentionally separate from evidence. After the full sitemap batch, 287 completed fixtures are `unsearched`, 12 are `archive_batch_scanned`, and five are `targeted_game_searched`. The nine future fixtures are `future_fixture` with `not_yet_applicable`. Sixteen completed fixtures have `partial_verified` evidence with 33 validated facts. This includes 2023 Minnesota, Michigan, and Maryland reports, plus 2024 season-ending carry-forward evidence for Teddy Prochazka and Barret Liebentritt across later 2024 fixtures. The other completed games remain unknown. Unknown is not healthy, and no box-score absence or unlisted player was mapped to an availability status.

Candidate rows therefore remain quarantined rather than being guessed into facts.

`retrospective_candidates.json` preserves additional 2023–2024 candidates found through public search, including the SI Illinois and Rutgers availability reports and the 2023 pre-Illinois Rhule press conference. Their text and opponent mapping are useful for review, but the SI origins currently return HTTP 410 and the 2023 official transcript lacks publisher version timestamps; they are excluded from pregame facts until an archive capture supplies the required proof.

## Reproduction

`build_ledger.py` rebuilds the full schedule-first ledger and records the pinned schedule hash. `batch_fetch.py` fetches a bounded URL list and caches raw responses. `extract_official.py` derives deterministic official candidates from a cached sitemap. `wp_paginate.py` paginates the public HuskerMax API. `compile_huskermax.py` deduplicates posts and writes the review inventory without admitting facts. `finalize_ledger.py` records the final search/evidence state.

The evidence output is deliberately conservative: `facts.json` contains 33 admitted facts, while `quarantine.json`, `quarantine_2023.json`, and `quarantine_boston.json` retain unresolved identity/timing candidates. `perarticle_review.json` gives a disposition to every one of the 266 HuskerCorner batch candidates, and `search_attempts.json` records every batch and count. This is systematic archive coverage for the discovered public routes; the ledger still shows 287 completed fixtures without a mapped game-specific attempt, and the 16 fact-bearing fixtures are partial evidence rather than complete team availability reports.
